> Physical AI is AI that perceives, reasons, and acts in the physical world. This chapter defines the perception → reasoning → action loop, explains why physical AI is fundamentally different from cloud AI (constraints are physics, not cost), maps the industry landscape (smartphones, wearables, robots, autonomous vehicles, industrial IoT, smart cameras), and identifies the role archetypes and which companies hire for each. It closes by explaining where this book fits: the engineering side, not the research side.

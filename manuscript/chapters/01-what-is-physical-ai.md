> Physical AI is AI that perceives, reasons, and acts in the physical world. This chapter defines the perception → reasoning → action loop, explains why physical AI is fundamentally different from cloud AI (constraints are physics, not cost), maps the industry landscape, and identifies the role archetypes and which companies hire for each.

## Industry landscape (2025–2026)

- **Smartphones & wearables:** Google Pixel 9 (Tensor G4 + custom ISP/TPU), Apple Intelligence on A18/M4 (ANE), Samsung Galaxy S25 (Snapdragon 8 Elite, Hexagon NPU), Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses (Llama on-device)
- **Robotics:** Physical Intelligence (π₀), Google DeepMind (RT-2, Gemini Robotics), Tesla (Optimus), Figure AI (Figure 02), 1X (NEO), Agility (Digit), Boston Dynamics (Atlas + Spot)
- **Autonomous vehicles:** Waymo (5th-gen Driver), Tesla FSD (HW4, end-to-end neural planner), Cruise, Mobileye (EyeQ Ultra), Qualcomm (Snapdragon Ride), NVIDIA (DRIVE Thor)
- **Industrial & edge:** NVIDIA Isaac (factory robots), Amazon Astro/warehouse robots, Qualcomm IoT, Google Coral (Edge TPU)
- **Chips & toolchains:** Qualcomm (QNN SDK, AI Hub), Apple (Core ML, ANE), Google (LiteRT, AI Edge), MediaTek (NeuroPilot), NVIDIA (TensorRT, Isaac Sim)
